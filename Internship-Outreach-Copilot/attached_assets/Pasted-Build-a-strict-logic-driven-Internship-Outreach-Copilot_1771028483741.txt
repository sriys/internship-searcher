Build a strict, logic-driven Internship Outreach Copilot.

Tech stack:
- Python 3
- Flask
- SQLite
- pandas + openpyxl
- requests + BeautifulSoup4
- OpenAI API (use OPENAI_API_KEY from environment variables)

No design needed. Minimal HTML templates only.

PRIMARY GOAL:
Convert accurate company website content into:
1) A validated research pack (fact-based, with evidence)
2) A strictly structured 120–155 word cold email
3) Enforce cooldowns and anti-spam safeguards

==============================
RESEARCH SYSTEM (STRICT MODE)
==============================

Step 1: Website Fetcher

For a given company URL:
- Restrict crawling to same domain only
- Fetch up to 6 pages max:
  - homepage
  - /about
  - /careers
  - /news OR /blog OR /press OR /insights
  - first "services" or "what we do" link found
  - first additional relevant internal link

If page not found, skip.
Strip:
- navigation
- footer
- cookie banners
- scripts
Keep only:
- headings
- paragraph text
Limit combined text to 18,000 characters max.

==============================
STEP A: FACT EXTRACTION PROMPT
==============================

Use this exact prompt when calling OpenAI:

"You are a fact-extraction assistant. Use ONLY the provided page text. Do NOT add outside knowledge.

Create exactly 4 research items.

For each item output:
- fact: one specific sentence
- evidence: a 6–18 word quote copied verbatim from provided text
- source_id: page key (home/about/careers/news/services/etc.)
- use_in_email: true or false

Rules:
- Fact must be grounded in the evidence quote.
- No numbers, dates, deals, rankings, clients unless explicitly in evidence.
- Prefer describing what the company does, who they serve, where they operate, or what they value.
- Avoid vague marketing claims unless explicitly quoted.
- Output valid JSON:
{
  "items": [ ...4 objects... ]
}"

Store:
- First 2 items where use_in_email=true
- Save fact and source_id
- If fewer than 2 usable facts, mark ResearchConfidence = LOW and block email generation.

==============================
STEP B: EMAIL GENERATION PROMPT
==============================

Use this exact prompt:

"You write short cold emails. Use ONLY the inputs provided. Do NOT invent facts.

Write an email with EXACTLY 6 paragraphs. Each paragraph must be 1–2 sentences.
Total length: 120–155 words.

Inputs:
contact_name: {CONTACT}
company_name: {COMPANY}
company_sentence: {FACT_FROM_RESEARCH}
track: {ib/consulting/econ_research/data/general}
company_size: {small/mid/large/unknown}
proof_line: {USER_PROOF_LINE}

Email Structure:
1) Greeting + who I am (high school senior in IB program in Glen Allen VA).
2) Include company_sentence exactly as written.
3) Include proof_line exactly as written.
4) Ask (based on company_size):
   - small/unknown: shadowing or small project-based opportunity
   - mid: project-based support or shadowing
   - large: brief conversation and guidance for high school path
5) Routing sentence EXACTLY:
   "If you're not the right person, who would you recommend I contact?"
6) Close with opt-out EXACTLY:
   "If you'd prefer I not follow up, I will respect that."

Return JSON:
{
  "subject_options": [3 short subject lines],
  "email_body": "full email"
}
No extra commentary."

==============================
VALIDATOR SYSTEM (MANDATORY)
==============================

After generating email:

1) Check word count between 120 and 155.
2) Ensure exactly 6 paragraphs separated by blank lines.
3) Ensure paragraph 2 contains company_sentence exactly.
4) Ensure paragraph 3 contains proof_line exactly.
5) Ensure routing sentence matches exactly.
6) Ensure opt-out line matches exactly.

7) Anti-hallucination rule:
If email contains:
- numbers not in company_sentence
- words like "recent deal", "announced", "transaction", "clients", "ranked", "award", unless present in company_sentence
→ Regenerate with stricter instruction: "Do not include any claims beyond company_sentence."

If validation fails, regenerate once.
If still fails, block draft and show error.

==============================
COOLDOWN RULES
==============================

- Max 1 initial email per company per 75 days
- Followup1 = sent date + 5 business days
- Followup2 = followup1 + 10 business days
- Stop after Followup2
- If DoNotContact = true, block generation

==============================
OUTPUT STORAGE
==============================

Store per company:
- Research Bullet 1
- Research Source 1
- Research Bullet 2
- Research Source 2
- ResearchConfidence
- Subject Line
- Email Draft
- Status
- LastContactDate
- Followup1Date
- Followup2Date
- CooldownUntil

==============================
DO NOT:
==============================

- Do not auto-send emails.
- Do not scrape LinkedIn.
- Do not add external facts not in website text.
- Do not exceed 6 crawled pages.

Deliver:
- Flask app
- Minimal HTML templates
- Clear comments
- Working research + strict email generator + validator
